Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet connection to use Score Keeper?

No account, no sign-in, and no internet required. Score Keeper works completely offline — your games, players, and scores stay on your device, private and always available.

How many players can I track, and can I name them?

Score Keeper handles 1 to 8 players in a game, each with a custom name and color. When a game wraps up it's saved to your history, so you can revisit past sessions and pick up where you left off anytime.

Can I save a game setup to reuse later?

Yes. Save your favorite configurations as templates so you can jump back into any game in seconds. No need to re-enter player names or settings every time you play.

Will I lose my game history if I delete the app?

Game history is stored locally on your device, so uninstalling the app removes it. Note down or screenshot anything you want to keep before deleting.

Why are there ads, and can I control them?

Score Keeper is free to download and use, and ads help keep it that way. You can adjust ad personalization anytime in Settings → Ad Preferences, and full details are in the Privacy Policy.
